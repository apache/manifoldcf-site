About This Blog

The Apache ManifoldCF Blog is the official platform for technical articles, architectural deep dives, release announcements, and integration guides written by the ManifoldCF committers and community.

Here is what you can expect to find on this blog:

  • Release Highlights & Roadmap: Overview of new features, performance improvements, and connector updates.
  • Connector Deep Dives: In-depth articles on configuring and customizing repository, authority, and output connectors.
  • Security Trimming Best Practices: How to properly configure Authority Services to maintain strict document-level security across enterprise search systems.
  • Community Spotlights & Tutorials: Step-by-step guides for extending ManifoldCF and developing custom plugins.

Have a topic you would like us to cover, or want to contribute an article? Reach out to our team on the Mailing Lists!
